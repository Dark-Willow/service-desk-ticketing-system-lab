## Objective

The objective of this lab is to understand how service desk tickets are classified using fields such as status, priority, impact, urgency, category, sub-category, technician, and support group.

## What I Learned

This lab helped me understand how ticket classification supports service desk workflow.

Status shows where the ticket is in the support process. Priority helps show how important the ticket is. Impact and urgency help decide how quickly the issue should be handled. Category and sub-category help organise tickets so they can be routed to the correct team or technician.

Using these fields properly makes tickets clearer, easier to track, and easier to manage in a service desk environment.
