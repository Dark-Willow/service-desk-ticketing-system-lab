# Service Desk Ticket Lifecycle

This document summarises the ticket workflow practised in this lab.

## Ticket Lifecycle

```text
User reports issue
        ↓
Ticket is created
        ↓
Ticket is categorised
        ↓
Priority, urgency, and impact are set
        ↓
Technician and support group are assigned
        ↓
Ticket is moved to In Progress
        ↓
Troubleshooting notes are added
        ↓
Resolution is documented
        ↓
Ticket is marked as Resolved
        ↓
User or requester confirmation is recorded
        ↓
Ticket is Closed
